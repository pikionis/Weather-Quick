Why ours should beat the source

  1. The source misses Kos in a way that repeats. Over the last 21 days its maximum ran 0.5 to 0.7 °C too warm, depending on how far ahead it looked.
  2. We subtract that miss for each lead day, using only what was known on the day each forecast was made.
  3. We replay the last 90 days as if they were live. Ours is shown only where it came out at least 0.1 °C better over 10 days or more. Everywhere else we show the source and say so.

Truth is ERA5: a 25 km cell, mostly sea and the Bodrum coast. Ours gets closer to ERA5, not to a station in Kos town.
