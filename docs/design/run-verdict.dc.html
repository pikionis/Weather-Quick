How we score

For someone who runs 30 to 90 minutes outdoors in Kos.

Each hour starts at 100 and loses points for rain, wind, heat and humidity. The day's score is its best two hours in the time you chose.

Good is 75 and up, OK 50 to 74, Poor under 50.
